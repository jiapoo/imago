## 3. TidBITS (Adam Engst)

```
Hi Adam,

Imago backs up an iPhone's photos and videos to the user's own Mac over the local network, then helps free up space on the iPhone safely.

The problem: a full iPhone usually leaves two choices, paying for more iCloud storage every month or deleting photos and hoping a copy exists somewhere. Many people with a Mac at home would rather keep their library there, but copying by cable with Image Capture or Photos is manual, and they never know which photos are safe to delete afterwards.

How it differs: Imago runs by itself, usually overnight while the phone charges, and saves originals as plain files in dated folders on the Mac or any drive you choose, readable without Imago. Cleanup happens on the Mac (duplicates, similar shots, bursts, large videos, screenshots), and the iPhone only deletes items the Mac holds a verified copy of, with the standard iOS confirmation and Recently Deleted as a safety net. There's no server and no account; the phone and Mac pair once with a QR code and talk over TLS 1.3 with a key made at pairing. Wi-Fi copy apps such as PhotoSync exist, but they're general transfer tools; Imago is built around keeping a complete, verified library on your Mac and deleting from the phone only when that's true.

As with Elapsa, I designed Imago and wrote it with heavy help from Claude Code; the problem, the design and every decision are mine.

Details:
- Apps: Imago: Back Up to Your Mac (iPhone and iPad) and Imago: iPhone Photo Backup (Mac, free)
- Developer: LJ Workshop, one person
- Requires: iOS 18 or later; macOS 15 or later; 10 languages
- Price: every feature free for 7 days, then a one-time $14.99 (Family $39.99 with Family Sharing). No subscription. Restoring photos is always free.
- Release: in App Review now; out around 1 December 2026
- Website: https://ljworkshop.com/imago/
- To try it now (in-app purchases are free in TestFlight, so no code is needed):
  iPhone https://testflight.apple.com/join/r3wdCbVp
  Mac https://testflight.apple.com/join/TMqb2bbP
- Press kit: https://ljworkshop.com/press/imago/

To remove it afterwards: quit the Mac app and drag it to the Trash; your backups stay as normal files in the folder you chose.

Thanks for reading,
L. J
LJ Workshop
```
